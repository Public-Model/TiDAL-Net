# Review-stage restricted-release notice

This repository accompanies a manuscript that is currently under revision and peer review. It is an interim technical release and is not the final paper-exact reproducibility archive.

Selected key parameters, exact experimental manifests, final checkpoints, training logs, processed data files, and the complete datasets used in the paper are temporarily withheld during review. Runnable substitutions are marked `REVIEW_DEFAULT` or `AUTHOR_REQUIRED` and must not be interpreted as the exact values used to generate the revised manuscript results.

After acceptance, the authors plan to publish the complete paper-exact code, configurations, manifests, checkpoints, experiment-reproduction scripts, and all datasets that may legally and contractually be redistributed. For third-party or restricted datasets that cannot be redistributed directly, the final release will provide exact source versions, access instructions, checksums, preprocessing scripts, channel manifests, split files, and other reproducibility metadata.

The authors should not label any current configuration as paper-exact until all items in `docs/AUTHOR_CHECKLIST.md` have been verified against the scripts, data, and checkpoints used for the accepted manuscript.
